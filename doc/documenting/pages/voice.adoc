= Voice

endif::[]

How a page reads: what goes into the prose and what stays out. Each rule shows a
weak and a strong version, both from a real review. The structure of books and
pages is in link:writing.html[Writing].

* **Show, do not describe**: when an example or a mockup shows a thing, the prose
  does not walk through it again. Keep only what the example cannot show, such
  as a rule.

* **Say what, not how**: A user guide tells what an application/tool does for
  the reader. How it does it belongs to a developer guide or to the code.

* **No history**: A page describes what is, not what it replaced or how it came
  to be. Where a direction matters, a short vision says where it goes.

* **Short captions**: A caption names the figure or table; the prose around it
  explains.
