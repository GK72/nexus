Heimdall is envisioned as a central place to track all kind of needs of a user:
personal tasks, development tasks, planner, calendar, reminders, etc.

== Planned

* **Weather** in the dashboard's header, in place of the placeholder.
* **Calendar**: the next four weeks in the dashboard.
* **Norn**: the planned and spent hours in the calendar, and the ready tasks
  per project, from `norn.py --json`.
* **Extended Git**:
** stale branches that are merged or idle, besides those whose upstream is
  gone;
** prunable worktrees, whose directory is gone;
** a record of the fetches, which keeps a failed fetch reported after the run:
  Git writes `FETCH_HEAD` even when a fetch fails;
** `--json` output, and `-a` for every repository and branch;
** a progress bar while fetching.
