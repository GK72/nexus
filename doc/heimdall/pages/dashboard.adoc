`heimdall` without a command prints the dashboard, the welcome screen of a
shell: the shell window of `tools/tmux.sh` runs `heimdall -f` when a session
starts. The screen is a header and a few sections, each under a rule with its
title, at most 100 columns wide.

A meter is green below 60%, yellow below 85% and red above.

== Repos

The brief report of link:git.html[Git], at most eight repositories, then how
many more there are. The rule carries the totals: the number of repositories,
how many need attention and the age of the oldest fetch, or `fetched just now`
after `-f`. When none needs attention, a single line says so.

== Footer

The time the dashboard took to gather everything, right-aligned and faint, so a
slow section shows.

== Options

.Options of `heimdall`
[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Option | Effect

| `-f`, `--fetch`   | fetch every repository before the survey
| `--repos N`       | repositories listed at most (default 8)
| `--width N`       | columns (default: the terminal's, at most 100)
| `--color WHEN`    | `auto` (default), `always` or `never`
| `-h`, `--help`    | the usage
| `--version`       | the version
|===
